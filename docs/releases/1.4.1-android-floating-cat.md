**GizeCat 1.4.1 — Android Floating Cat Preview.** An **Android-only** update: GizeCat for Android tablets can now float over your launcher and other apps. **This is a debug-signed preview, not a production release and not a Google Play build, and it has not been run on any Android device, emulator or simulator.**

## What is new

- A native **floating cat**: once you explicitly allow it, a small transparent window follows the cat over other apps. It walks along the bottom of the screen, above the navigation bar, plays, sleeps and reacts as in the app, and you can touch, pet, pick up, drag and throw it.
- You control it: **Pause**, **Resume**, **Hide**, **Show**, **Stop**, and open the settings, from the app and from the notification Android requires while it floats.
- It never starts by itself, not even after a reboot, and the app works fully without it.

## Required permission

The floating cat needs Android's **Display over other apps** permission (`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`). The app explains it first, and opens Android's own settings page only when you press **Open Android settings**; you turn it on yourself and can turn it off at any time. It also declares a foreground service and, on Android 13+, asks once to show its notification. The app reads nothing from other apps, uses no accessibility service, no screen capture and no network.

## Download and install

`GizeCat-1.4.1-android-trial-debugsigned.apk` (with `SHA256SUMS.txt`). Copy it to your tablet, open it, allow "Install unknown apps" for the app you opened it from, and install. It installs as **GizeCat (trial)**, application id `io.github.gizeverse.gizecat.trial`, version 1.4.1 (versionCode 10402).

- It is signed with the Android **debug** key, the same one as the 1.4.0 trial APK (certificate SHA-256 `162fffa6298efb7c668a7b4f41ae87720c90caa383b460449737e53dadcd53a1`), so it **updates the 1.4.0 trial APK in place** and keeps your cat.
- It is **not guaranteed to update in place to a future production-signed build**; you may have to uninstall first. Save your cat to a file first (Settings, **Take your cat to another device**).
- Steps, grant and revoke, upgrade and uninstall: [docs/platforms/android-overlay.md](../platforms/android-overlay.md).

## Known limits

- **Not runtime-verified.** Built and statically inspected only; never run on a physical Android device, an emulator or a simulator, and no test was run. Expect rough edges.
- **Touch pass-through is limited by Android's overlay rules.** The window is only the cat and a margin (about 172 × 196 dp at default settings); touches outside it reach the app underneath, touches in the margin are consumed. While the cat is paused it lets touches through at 80 % opacity (untried).
- **Floor-only movement.** Other apps' windows are invisible to an app, so the cat walks on the screen floor, though you can drag and throw it anywhere.
- **Some secure or system screens** (permission dialogs, some banking and security apps) hide overlays or block touches while one is above them. Hide the cat from the notification there.
- The tail's inertia and the held cat's swing are reduced in the floating window.
- **No claim of Google Play approval.** The overlay and foreground-service declarations are drafted, not filed.

## What this release does not change

- **Windows stays on the 1.4.0 Preview** (`Desktop-Cat-Setup-1.4.0-x64.exe` and `-arm64.exe`); no Windows binary is updated or attached here, and the desktop version in the repository is still 1.4.0.
- macOS and iPadOS are not part of this update.
- The v1.4.0 Preview and its assets are untouched.
