# GizeCat 1.5.0, Living Companion (release notes DRAFT)

**Draft for the owner to review. 1.5.0 is a release candidate: not published, not tagged.** The current published releases are Windows v1.3.0 and Android v1.4.2. Windows and Android only; macOS and iPad are not part of this release.

**Nothing in 1.5.0 has been run.** No test, emulator, simulator or application launch was performed for any 1.5.0 code. Everything below is IMPLEMENTED and statically verified (type-check, lint, read-only review); the Windows installers and Android files are COMPILED_OR_PACKAGED at most. Remove or soften nothing in the "What is verified" section without a run to point to.

## Headline

**GizeCat's cat now has a personality you choose, moods you can see, and a small world that visits it.** Seven personalities plus Auto, a mood the cat shows, world events (a mouse, a butterfly, a little storm), Laser Mode, a private Cat Journal, reduced motion and optional sound cues. Every part can be switched off, and nothing new is collected or sent.

## What is new

### Living Companion behaviour
- **Mood** (Happy, Curious, Sleepy, Startled, Scared, Excited, Relaxed, Confused), derived from what the cat is doing and shown in its expression, and a **deeper sleep** the longer it sleeps.
- The simulation stays the source of truth: world events and the laser ask the cat to do things through its ordinary behaviours, and your drag, throw, tap and petting always win.

### Animation system
- New **scratch**, **shake** and **confused** clips, mood expression layers and deep sleep, drawn by the same procedural renderer.

### Personality and memory
- **Seven selectable personalities plus Auto:** Curious, Playful, Energetic, Lazy, Shy, Mischievous, Calm. Auto keeps the traits the cat was born with. The cat never changes your choice.
- A **bounded local world memory**: the last 16 events and up to 60 journal entries, on your device.
- **Shy** cats watch a new event for a few seconds before coming closer; **Lazy** cats often let small events (a falling leaf, bubbles) pass. Mischievous is a temperament, not a mood. The cat's per-event counters give it a sense of which events are familiar or new.

### World events
- **20 everyday and 6 rare events**, with small pictures on the overlay. One happens after a random wait of 5 to 30 minutes, counted from the end of the previous event; pace can be halved or doubled (never closer than 2 minutes). Recent events are not repeated.
- **Rare events** come from a separate pool, about 6 times in 100 due events. The Midnight Visitor appears only between 22:00 and 05:00.
- Props are pictures only: **never clickable**, never part of the click-through region. A running event winds down if you interact, type heavily, pause the cat, hide it or a full-screen app comes in front.
- Switch all events off, rare events off, any single event off, or pause them for the session.

### Laser Mode
- A red dot the cat chases and **never catches**. Off at every start, never saved.
- **Windows:** the dot is drawn on the overlay at your real pointer position; your system cursor is untouched.
- **Android:** only touches and hover that Android delivers to the cat's small touch window, and position hints from GizeCat's own app window. No global tracking, no new permission.

### Journal
- A quiet diary of memorable moments, on your device only, newest 60 kept, with a switch and a **Clear journal** button (it deletes the entries, the short event history and the event counters; the cat and its other memories stay). No streaks, scores or reminders. Never part of a saved profile.

### UI
- **Windows Settings:** new **Companion**, **Audio**, **Accessibility & Focus** and **Journal** pages; the seven-profile chooser on **Personality**; world-event settings in **Behaviour**; **Laser mode** in the tray and the cat's menu.
- **Android home panel:** Companion, Personality, Behavior (world events), Interactions (Laser mode), Audio, Accessibility and Journal sections.
- **Reduced motion** (also follows the system setting) and separate **Cat sounds** and **World sounds** switches (audio stays off by default).

## Platform notes

- **Windows 10/11:** x64 and ARM64 installers. The 1.3.0 baseline ran on Windows 11 hardware; the 1.4.0 Preview and 1.5.0 never ran, and ARM64 has never run on Arm hardware.
- **Android phones and tablets:** the floating cat from 1.4.2 (overlay, drag and throw, edge climbing, touch, rotation, panel, app switching) was **OWNER-VERIFIED on one physical Android phone**. Tablets and other phones are not individually verified, and none of the 1.5.0 Android code has run on a device.
- Same four Android permissions as 1.4.2.

## How to install

Install only if you trust this source and accept that nothing here has been run by its builders.

**Windows.** Download `Desktop-Cat-Setup-1.5.0-x64.exe` (or `-arm64.exe` for a Windows on Arm PC), check its SHA-256 against `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and run it. The installer is **unsigned**: SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC" (More info, Run anyway) and Smart App Control may block it. There is no publisher verification. It installs per user, and is built to keep your cat in `%APPDATA%\Desktop Cat` as earlier versions do (an upgrade to 1.5.0 was not tested). Quit the running cat first.

**Android.** Download `GizeCat-1.5.0-android-trial-debugsigned.apk`, check its SHA-256, allow "Install unknown apps" for the app you opened it from, and install. It is signed with the Android **Debug** certificate, installs as **GizeCat (trial)**, and updates earlier trial APKs in place. It is not a Google Play package, and Play Protect may warn; static review does not prove an app is safe. The permission steps are those of 1.4.2 ([android-overlay.md](../platforms/android-overlay.md)).

**Why the release APK and AAB are unsigned.** `GizeCat-1.5.0-android-unsigned.apk` and `GizeCat-1.5.0-android-unsigned.aab` were built without a release key, because the owner's key is not in the build. An unsigned APK cannot be installed. The AAB is for Google Play after the owner signs it; no Play listing exists and no approval is claimed ([signing](../platforms/android-signing.md)).

## Build provenance and verification state

- **Source commit:** the artifacts above were built from `431b8c6dd0990e10a0d6e5398ca1a7fdb5ba5654` on `feature/living-companion`, from a clean tree (0 uncommitted files at the start, at the resume and at the end of the build). The commit that records these notes differs from `431b8c6` only under `docs/` (identical git tree hashes for `src`, `android`, `scripts`, `build`, the package files, `electron-builder.yml` and the notices). Details and the full provenance record: [artifact inventory](1.5.0-artifact-inventory.md). An earlier build of the same version from `f8fe919` was superseded and none of its hashes is reused.
- **Static checks that passed:** `tsc` for the node and web projects and `eslint` on `src` and `tests` (exit 0); two read-only adversarial reviews (all confirmed defects fixed); static payload checks of both Windows builds; `aapt2`, `apksigner` and archive inspection of the Android files (application ids, versionCode 10500, exactly four permissions, no native libraries, signing states).
- **Not performed:** every kind of runtime check. No test was run (including `verify fast`, which runs the suite), no installer was run, no application was launched, no emulator, simulator or device was used. All Living Companion behaviour is IMPLEMENTED and statically verified only. The unit tests written for it have never executed.
- **Signing:** the Windows installers are **unsigned** (no code-signing certificate exists); the Android trial APK carries the Android **debug** certificate; the release APK and AAB are **unsigned**. Android production signing stays blocked until you establish a permanent, owner-controlled keystore ([signing readiness](1.5.0-signing-readiness.md)).
- **Publication:** **no tag and no public release were created** for 1.5.0; nothing was uploaded; `main` was not touched.

## Known limitations and issues

- **Never run.** Expect defects, especially in drawing props and the laser dot, the Android stage window, event timing and the new settings pages.
- **Android Laser Mode** cannot follow a finger over other apps; it needs Hunt and pounce on.
- A small area around the Android cat consumes touches (a platform limit carried over from 1.4.2).
- Windows installers and the Android trial APK are not signed by a publisher; the release APK and AAB are unsigned.
- Windows 10, multi-monitor and mixed-DPI hardware, and Windows on Arm remain untested.

## Privacy and permission changes

**None.** Screen analysis and cloud access stay off by default. No telemetry, keyboard hook or clipboard capture. The laser pointer position only draws the dot and is never stored, logged or sent. The journal stays on the device. Android declares the same four permissions and no network permission.
