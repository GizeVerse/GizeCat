GizeCat

A GizeVerse character, living in your browser tab.

What it does here

The cat lives along the bottom of this page: it wanders, naps, grooms and keeps an eye on your pointer. Stroke it back and forth to pet it, click or tap it, or drag it to pick it up and let go to drop or throw it. On a touch screen, press and hold it for a moment before you drag.

The rest of the page keeps working: only the cat itself takes your clicks and taps. It needs no account, makes no network requests and never listens to your keyboard. It remembers itself in this browser's local storage.
